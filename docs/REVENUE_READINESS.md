# HONET Revenue Readiness

## Objective

Turn the Question Engine into a measurable commercial experiment without claiming revenue or customer demand before validation.

## Product

HONET converts observations, documents, and notebook images into structured observations and ranked research questions. The commercial hypothesis is that teams will pay when the engine reliably produces more useful, testable questions than a standard LLM workflow.

## Validation funnel

1. **Technical validity** — deterministic scoring and API integration work.
2. **Comparative validity** — benchmark HONET against a baseline LLM on identical inputs.
3. **User validity** — collect ratings from target users on question usefulness.
4. **Willingness to pay** — test a small paid pilot before building a large SaaS surface.
5. **Repeatability** — demonstrate value on multiple domains.

## Initial pricing hypotheses

These are experiments, not forecasts:

| Offer | Hypothesis | Starting test price |
|---|---|---:|
| Explorer | Individual evaluation | Free |
| Researcher | Higher-volume analysis | USD 29/month |
| Team Pilot | Customized R&D workflow | USD 199/month |

Prices should change based on observed willingness to pay and delivered value.

## Customer evidence required

Before treating the product as commercially validated, record:

- customer problem statement
- target user/persona
- example input
- baseline workflow
- HONET output
- measurable improvement
- user feedback
- willingness-to-pay signal
- objections
- next action

## Revenue readiness checklist

- [x] Working Question Engine prototype

## Guardrails

HONET must distinguish observations, hypotheses, and evidence. It must not claim a question is novel, a hypothesis is true, or a customer will pay without supporting evidence.
